# Tobi's Digital Canvas

Create a polished, production-quality one-page personal portfolio website for Oluwatobi Bada, a Nigerian founder, product builder, and entrepreneur.

Design direction:
- Editorial, premium, modern founder portfolio
- Warm off-white background (#F3F0E8), nearly-black text (#151513), bright orange accent (#FF5A26)
- Bold oversized typography, lots of whitespace, subtle thin borders, elegant serif italics for emphasis
- Responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Smooth anchor scrolling, accessible labels and keyboard-friendly navigation
- Use tasteful CSS geometric orbit lines and cards; avoid generic gradients and generic SaaS styling

Page structure:
1. Sticky navigation: OB. logo, About, Work, Contact, and a “Let's talk” button.
2. Hero:
   Eyebrow: “Founder · Product Builder · Entrepreneur”
   Headline: “I build digital products that make everyday life simpler.”
   Intro: “I'm Oluwatobi Bada, a founder turning ambitious ideas into useful digital platforms for people and businesses across Africa.”
   Buttons: “Explore my work” and “More about me”
   Add a refined arched portrait card on the right. For now use an elegant neutral placeholder that is easy to replace with Oluwatobi's real portrait, with label “Oluwatobi Bada — Founder & Product Builder.” Do not use a random person's face.
3. About section on dark background:
   Heading: “Ideas are only the beginning. I care about making them work.”
   Copy explaining he is an entrepreneur and product builder focused on practical technology across business, logistics, experiences, and digital innovation.
   Skills: Product Strategy, Business Development, Digital Platforms, Brand Building.
4. Selected Work:
   - VenueDek: “Find the right place for every moment.” A discovery and booking platform connecting people with venues, activities, and memorable experiences. Link to https://venuedek.com
   - MoveDek: “Moving things forward.” A technology-powered logistics platform making local delivery and moving simpler, more transparent, and dependable. Link to https://movedek.com
   Use distinctive orange and muted green visual cards with abstract CSS artwork.
5. How I Think: three principles — “Start with the problem,” “Keep it useful,” “Build for the long term,” with concise explanatory copy.
6. Contact:
   Heading: “Have an idea worth building?”
   Text: “Open to partnerships, opportunities, and thoughtful conversations.”
   Include email and LinkedIn call-to-action placeholders clearly marked for later editing; do not invent contact information.
7. Footer: “© 2026 Oluwatobi Bada — Building useful things from Nigeria.”

Build it as a complete React + TypeScript app using Tailwind and shadcn/ui where helpful. Make all buttons and navigation work. Ensure there are no broken links, overflow, or console errors.
